Before You Get Started

⚠️ Gratuities Notice

Gratuities are NOT included in your cabin price. These are a separate cost payable directly to the cruise line.

📋 Action Required — Cabin Travelers

You are required to add all travelers sharing your cabin using the Cabin Travelers section on your dashboard. This information is required for cruise check-in and must be completed before your first payment.

Complete Your Profile

📋 Travel Documentation — U.S. Citizens

First and last names on cruise and airline reservations must match your government-issued ID.

  • Recently married guests — book under maiden name unless you have official name-change documents.
  • Traveling with a birth certificate + government ID — bring legal name-change documentation if names differ.
⚠️ Traveling with Minors

If a minor is traveling without one or both parents/guardians, bring a signed letter from the absent parent(s) authorizing travel.

📎 Travel Documentation: U.S. Citizens ↗

Guests are personally responsible for their documents. The trip organizer assumes no responsibility.

📋 Travel Documentation — U.S. Permanent Residents

U.S. Permanent Residents must travel with a valid Permanent Resident Card (Green Card) and a valid passport from their country of citizenship.

  • A passport valid for at least six months beyond the completion of travel is strongly recommended.
  • Additional entry requirements may apply depending on the ports of call on your itinerary.
📎 Travel Documentation: U.S. Permanent Residents ↗

Guests are personally responsible for their documents. The trip organizer assumes no responsibility.

📋 Travel Documentation — Non-U.S. Citizens

Guests traveling without proper documentation will be denied boarding with no refund. A passport valid for at least six months beyond travel completion is strongly recommended.

  • VWP eligible travelers — valid passport + ESTA required.
  • Non-VWP citizens — a valid U.S. visa is required.
  • Mexican citizens with a Border Crossing Card must also present a valid Mexican passport.
📎 Travel Documentation: Non-U.S. Citizens ↗

Guests are personally responsible for their documents. The trip organizer assumes no responsibility.

📋 Travel Documentation — Canadian Citizens

All Canadian citizens, including children, must carry a valid Canadian passport (at least six months beyond travel completion if arriving by air into the U.S.).

  • Expedited passports: visacentral.ca ↗ or 888-665-9956
📎 Travel Documentation: Canadian Citizens ↗

Guests are personally responsible for their documents. The trip organizer assumes no responsibility.
