Citizenship & Travel Documentation

📋 U.S. Citizens — Travel Documentation

Names on reservations must match your unexpired government-issued photo ID.

  • Recently married: Book under maiden name unless you have name-change documents.
  • Birth certificate + ID: Bring name-change documentation if names differ.
⚠️ Minors Traveling Without Both Parents

Bring a signed letter from the absent parent(s) authorizing travel.

📎 U.S. Citizens Travel Documentation ↗ (opens link — then you may check below)
Guests are personally responsible for their documents.

📋 U.S. Permanent Residents — Travel Documentation

Must carry a valid Permanent Resident Card (Green Card) and a valid passport from their country of citizenship.

  • Passport valid for at least six months beyond travel completion strongly recommended.
  • Additional entry requirements may apply at ports of call.
📎 U.S. Permanent Residents Documentation ↗ (opens link — then you may check below)
Guests are personally responsible for their documents.

📋 Non-U.S. Citizens — Travel Documentation

Traveling without proper documentation will result in denial of boarding with no refund. Passport valid 6+ months beyond travel is strongly recommended.

  • VWP eligible: Valid passport + ESTA required.
  • Non-VWP: Valid U.S. visa required.
  • Mexican citizens with a Border Crossing Card must also carry a valid Mexican passport.
📎 Non-U.S. Citizens Documentation ↗ (opens link — then you may check below)
Guests are personally responsible for their documents.

📋 Canadian Citizens — Travel Documentation

All Canadian citizens, including children, must carry a valid Canadian passport (6+ months beyond travel if arriving by air into the U.S.).

  • Expedited passports: visacentral.ca ↗ or 888-665-9956
📎 Canadian Citizens Documentation ↗ (opens link — then you may check below)
Guests are personally responsible for their documents.

📋 Other Citizenship — Travel Documentation

The trip organizer will follow up with documentation requirements specific to your country. Please ensure the country of citizenship is entered correctly above.

📎 Guests are personally responsible for all required travel documents. The trip organizer assumes no responsibility.
